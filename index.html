Flight Broom

A broomstick you sit on and fly: steer it by its handles, and the right stick sets the speed. Made for SideQuest / AltspaceVR worlds.

It works at any level (it doesn't use bird flight), in VR and on PC. Open flight-broom.firer.at as a space in Altspace to try it.

How to fly

In VR

  1. Click the broom to sit on it.
  2. Hold its handles with one hand or both: lift them to climb, push them down to dive, move them left or right to turn. The left stick steers too: forward dives, back climbs, left and right turn. The broom tilts you with it, and lifting or pushing rises or sinks you even when you're still.
  3. Push the right stick up to speed up, down to slow down. Click it in to stop.
  4. Let go of the handles to hover.
  5. Press B (or jump) to get off.

On PC

  1. Click the broom to sit on it.
  2. W speeds up, S slows down, A and D turn, E climbs, and Q dives (straight up or down when you're still). The handles work too.
  3. Press Space to get off.

A broom waits where the world's builder put it, with a Click the broom to ride label over it. The first time you sit on one, a message shows the controls for VR or PC.

You have one broom out at a time: sitting on a broom sends your other one away. With sync="true" the brooms are shared, so everyone sees them, and sitting on one puts a new one at the spot. After you get off, it goes back to its spot 15 seconds later (a shared one goes away instead).

2a. Add it in Unity

You need a Unity project with the Altspace Creator SDK already set up, and your world's scene open.

  1. Make an empty object. In the top menu choose GameObject › Create Empty. Rename it to Flight Broom so you can find it later (the object's name doesn't matter, only the slug does).
  2. Put it at the middle of the world. With the new object selected, look at Transform in the Inspector and set Position to 0 0 0 and Rotation to 0 0 0. Why: you don't move the broom by moving this object. You move it with the snippet's own position setting (step 6). If the object (or anything it's inside) isn't at 0 0 0, the SDK's gizmo won't line up with where the broom really appears.
  3. Add the Snippet component. Click Add Component, type Snippet in the search box, and pick BS › Snippet.
  4. Type the slug and press Enter. In the Snippet's Slug box type and press Enter (the box only takes it after Enter or clicking somewhere else). It downloads the snippet and adds it to your world's Assets/WebRoot/index.html for you. After a moment the Inspector shows the title Flight Broom and a list of settings. If nothing happens, click Refresh from server.
  5. Save the scene. Press Ctrl + S (Windows / Linux) or Cmd + S (Mac). Do this every time you add or remove a snippet. Closing Unity without saving is the main way extra copies of the snippet end up in index.html.
  6. Place and configure it. Drag the gizmo handle in the Scene view to choose where the broom waits, and change the other settings in the Inspector. They're all listed under Settings.
  7. Upload your world the same way you always do, then join it in Altspace and click the broom to ride.

More brooms: add Flight Broom once, then give it more spots. Click Open index.html in the Snippet component and add broom-2-position="5 0.8 0", broom-3-position="0 20 4" and so on to the snippet. Each one is another broom, with the same settings. Use as many as you like. Add broom-2-seat-turn="90" to ride broom 2 side-saddle.

2b. Add it on the website

  1. Open your world for editing on altvr.app and go to the Snippets section.
  2. Find Flight Broom. Set the list to show All snippets, then search for Flight Broom (or scroll to it) and open it.
  3. Copy it. Click Copy & open index.html. This copies the snippet's code and opens your world's HTML (or copy the full snippet code below).
  4. Paste it in the right place. If your HTML has these two lines, paste between them: 
    <!-- snippet section -->
   paste here
<!-- end snippet section -->
    If it doesn't have them, paste it just before </body>.
  5. Change the settings by editing the values in quotes in the pasted code, for example position="0 0.8 2". For more brooms, add broom-2-position="5 0.8 0", broom-3-position="0 20 4" and so on, as many as you like.
  6. Save, then join your world and click the broom to ride.

Keep the code tidy. Every value needs quotes around it (max-speed="12", not max-speed=12), and a & in any link must be written as &amp;. Unity reads this part strictly, and one mistake can break the whole snippet section.

The full snippet code

This is everything the snippet adds, with every setting at its default. Click the code to copy it.

Full snippet code
<!-- snippet section -->
<bs-snippet name="broom" title="Flight Broom" single="true"
  description="A broomstick you sit on and fly: steer it by its handles, the right stick sets the speed."
  script="https://flight-broom.firer.at/snippet.js"
  position="0 0.8 -2"
  yaw="0"
  max-speed="12"
  accel="4"
  turn-speed="90"
  max-pitch="50"
  stick-angle="25"
  seat-turn="0"
  climb-speed="3"
  tilt="1"
  collide="true"
  steer-range="0.2"
  dead-zone="0.03"
  response="3"
  pitch-ease="3"
  return-after="15"
  help="true"
  label="true"
  label-text="Click the broom to ride"
  sync="false"
  sync-limit="10"
  bundle-base="">
  <bs-gizmo type="position" attribute="position"/>
  <bs-gizmo type="box" attribute="position" size="0.5 0.16 0.5"/>
</bs-snippet>
<!-- end snippet section -->

Settings

All optional. An empty or missing setting uses the default.

SettingDefaultWhat it does
position0 0.8 2Where the broom waits (a Unity world position, the top of its saddle), and where it goes back to.
broom-2-position, broom-3-position, ...(none)More brooms, each one x y z. Add as many as you like; they all use the settings below.
broom-2-seat-turn, broom-3-seat-turn, ...seat-turnseat-turn for that broom only, for example broom-2-seat-turn="90" for a side-saddle broom.
yaw0Which way the brooms face, in degrees about up.
max-speed12Top speed in metres per second.
accel4Metres per second gained or lost per second with the right stick (or W / S).
turn-speed90Degrees per second with the handles right over to the side.
max-pitch50Degrees the broom noses up or down with the handles right up or down.
stick-angle25Degrees the stick points up where it rests. Holding it there is level flight.
seat-turn0Degrees the seat (and you) turn from the broom. 90 or -90 rides side-saddle; the handles stay at the broom's front.
climb-speed3Metres per second straight up or down with the handles right up or down (or E / Q), on top of the speed, so you can rise or sink on the spot.
tilt1How much of the nose up or down tilts you on the seat: 0 keeps you upright, 1 tilts you all the way with the broom.
collidetruefalse lets the broom fly through walls, floors and other solid things.
steer-range0.2Metres past the dead zone for a full turn or climb.
dead-zone0.03Metres round where the handles rest that don't steer.
response3How quickly your speed follows, per second. Higher feels snappier, lower floatier.
pitch-ease3How quickly the broom noses up and down, per second.
return-after15Seconds after you get off before the broom goes back. false (or 0): brooms stay where you left them.
helptruefalse skips the message with the controls the first time someone sits on a broom.
labeltruefalse hides the Click the broom to ride label over the broom's spot.
label-textClick the broom to rideWhat the label over the brooms says. No double quotes.
broom-2-label-text, broom-3-label-text, ...label-textlabel-text for that broom only.
syncfalsetrue: the brooms are shared, so everyone sees them. Sitting on one puts a new shared broom at the spot; one you're done with goes away for everyone.
sync-limit10Most shared brooms out in the space at once. Past it, the broom at each spot is a local one.
respawn-delay3Seconds after a shared broom is taken before the next one appears at its spot.
bundle-base(empty)A folder URL to load the broom's bundles from instead of this site.

Good to know

  • Without sync, others see you flying but not the broom. With sync="true" the brooms are shared, up to sync-limit out at once; past that each spot has a local broom again.
  • Letting go of the handles leaves you hovering in place until you steer again or get off.
  • The broom stops at walls and floors, sliding along them. Set collide="false" to fly through them.
  • Your page's code can bring a broom to you: flightBroom.bring() puts the first broom in front of you, and flightBroom.bring(2) broom 2.
